### Task 1: Goal Conversion Efficiency ($\text{Gls}/\text{Sh}$) Across Tournament Stages

#### 1. Analytic Question Formulation

* **Investigation Goal:** Evaluate whether qualification for the knockout stage was driven by finishing precision rather than sheer shot volume.
* **Analytic Question:** Did national teams that advanced to the Knockout Stage achieve a significantly higher Goal Conversion Efficiency ($\text{Gls}/\text{Sh}$) than teams eliminated in the Group Stage during the FIFA World Cup 2026?

#### 2. Data Wrangling & Preparation

* **Datasets Merged:** `squad_shooting.csv` and `player_shooting.csv` (FBref).


* **Wrangling Operations:**
* Cleaned national squad identifiers using regex string stripping (removing country code prefixes).
* Converted match counts (`90s`), goals (`Gls`), shots (`Sh`), and conversion efficiency (`G/Sh`) into numeric data types.
* Aggregated player-level demographic indicators (`Avg_Squad_Age`, `Total_Players_Used`) and executed a left merge with squad statistics on key `Squad`.


* Filtered the dataset to $N = 48$ nations and exported the final consolidated file: `merged_fifa2026_squad_data.csv`.




* **Grouping Criterion:**
* **Group Stage Eliminated ($n_1 = 16$):** Teams that concluded their tournament at 3 group matches ($\text{90s} \le 3.0$).
* **Knockout Stage Qualified ($n_2 = 32$):** Teams that advanced to the Round of 32 and beyond ($\text{90s} > 3.0$).



#### 3. Descriptive Statistics & Visualization

* **Descriptive Summary Table:**

| Metric | Group Stage Eliminated ($n_1 = 16$) | Knockout Stage Qualified ($n_2 = 32$) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mean ($\bar{x}$)** | **0.0681** (6.81%) | **0.1253** (12.53%) |
| **Standard Deviation ($s$)** | **0.0395** | **0.0463** |
| **Median (Q2)** | **0.0600** (6.00%) | **0.1300** (13.00%) |
| **IQR (Q3 - Q1)** | **0.0725** ($0.0375 - 0.1100$) | **0.0525** ($0.0975 - 0.1500$) |
| **Min - Max** | **0.0000 - 0.1300** | **0.0400 - 0.2400** |

* **Visual Output:** A dual-distribution histogram saved to `task1_conversion_dist.png` visually highlights that the knockout group distribution is shifted distinctly to the right toward higher efficiency values.

#### 4. Inferential Statistics (Confidence Intervals)

* **Sampling Distribution Assumptions:** Because the true population variances are unknown and $n_1 = 16 < 30$, Student's $t^*$ distribution is applied ($df_1 = 15$, $df_2 = 31$).


* **Knockout Stage 95% CI:**

$$\bar{x}_2 \pm t^*_{0.025, 31} \times \frac{s_2}{\sqrt{n_2}} = 0.1253 \pm 2.040 \times 0.00818 \implies \mathbf{[0.1086, \, 0.1420]}$$


* **Group Stage Eliminated 95% CI:**

$$\bar{x}_1 \pm t^*_{0.025, 15} \times \frac{s_1}{\sqrt{n_1}} = 0.0681 \pm 2.131 \times 0.00988 \implies \mathbf{[0.0471, \, 0.0892]}$$


* **Interpretation:** The 95% confidence intervals do not overlap, providing clear evidence of a performance gap between the two cohorts.

#### 5. Inferential Statistics (Two-Sample Welch's $t$-Test)

* **Hypotheses:**
* $H_0: \mu_{\text{Knockout}} = \mu_{\text{Eliminated}}$ (No difference in mean conversion efficiency).


* $H_a: \mu_{\text{Knockout}} > \mu_{\text{Eliminated}}$ (Knockout teams have higher mean conversion efficiency).




* **Test Parameters:** Welch's Two-Sample $t$-test (`equal_var=False`, `alternative='greater'`).


* **Results:**
* **$t$-statistic ($t^*$):** **$4.4572$**

* **Degrees of Freedom ($df$):** $\approx 35.1$
* **$p$-value:** **$0.000041$ ($p < 0.0001$)**



* **Conclusion:** Because $p < 0.05$, we reject the null hypothesis $H_0$. Knockout-qualified teams converted shots into goals at almost double the efficiency of eliminated teams ($12.53\%$ vs. $6.81\%$), a difference that is statistically significant.
